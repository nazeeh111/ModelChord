# MODELCHORD feasibility study protocol v1

Registered on 2026-10-29 before fitting specialists or reading confirmation metrics. This is a bounded local study, not a foundation-model benchmark. CPU only, four Torch threads, no paid compute or APIs.

## Vision source and split

scikit-learn `load_digits` supplies 1,797 8x8 optical digit images, the original UCI **test subset**. We make a fresh controlled partition, so these are not results under the original benchmark test protocol. No outside pretrained weights. Credit: Alpaydin & Kaynak (1998), Optical Recognition of Handwritten Digits, DOI 10.24432/C50P49, CC BY 4.0, https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/80/optical+recognition+of+handwritten+digits .

Group exact pixel duplicates before splitting; every transformed view of one source remains in its partition. Stratified split fractions: 55% expert training, 10% permitted calibration/repair, 15% candidate selection, 20% untouched confirmation. Source IDs, dataset-content hash, split seed 20261029 and partition hashes are written before training. Writer IDs are unavailable in this packaged subset; no writer-disjoint claim.

Common representation: flattened row-major float32 8x8 pixels divided by 16. Common MLP: 64→96→64→10, ReLU hidden activations, ordered labels digit strings 0–9. Base is trained on clean training inputs. Three specialists start from exactly that base and train on one-column zero-filled left shift, one-column right shift, and inversion 1-x. Their head and labels stay identical. Every specialist must beat its base on its intended selection capability or be explicitly rejected. Seeds 11 and 29 create independently initialized bases/specialists. Seed 11 tunes merge scalars; seed 29 reuses those settings, a held-out expert-initialization check on the same source partition. Two seeds do not justify significance claims.

Floors declared before evaluation: clean 0.88; left, right, inverted each 0.80. Protected clean may regress at most 0.02 relative to the selected ordinary model. Empirical accuracy floors are finite-sample targets, not universal behavior guarantees. Base: Adam lr 0.006 for 160 full-batch epochs. Specialists: Adam lr 0.006 for 65 epochs, same training source IDs and transformed domain. No expert training uses calibration, selection or confirmation data.

## Baselines, selection, and repair

Execute base, individual experts, conventional task arithmetic, tuned TIES and DARE-style arithmetic, TSV (Task Singular Vectors Algorithm 1), activation-informed RegMean, probability ensemble cost reference, and ordinary full-weight fine-tuning. Scalar grid {0.25,0.5,0.75,1.0}; TIES/DARE density {0.2,0.5}. Register every attempted candidate. Select lexicographically by (worst floor deficit, sum deficits, mean cross entropy), never average quality alone. RegMean uses one calibration batch per expert, ridge 0.001, off-diagonal covariance factor 0.5, bias handled via constant-augmented features. TSV truncates each task SVD to floor(min(shape)/task_count), polar-orthogonalizes concatenated singular-vector bases and averages bias deltas; tiny-matrix fallbacks are disclosed. No claim that these baseline reimplementations equal every reference repository option.

Export and independently invoke the selected conventional task-arithmetic model before repair. Repair uses rank budget 8, at most 4 gradient rounds, intervention ranks {1,2}, normalized Frobenius step sizes {0.04,0.12,0.35}, at most 72 candidate interventions. The model's measured worst deficit chooses calibration gradient targets. Gradients propose subspaces; validation of actual controlled interventions ranks and gates steps. Only rank-limited matrix corrections change; biases remain frozen. A protected-task selection gate rejects damaging steps. This is labeled data-assisted optimization, not training-free merging. Fold accepted corrections into one static model. Parent experts are absent during deployed inference.

Ordinary joint fine-tuning begins at exactly the same selected task-arithmetic weights and consumes exactly the repair's gradient example-access sequence/count and gradient steps, with zero teacher calls. Full weights are trainable. Learning-rate grid {0.002,0.006,0.02} uses the same selection partition. Candidate evaluation counts and elapsed times are reported separately. This matches examples, optimization steps and teacher calls; it does not match exact FLOPs, parameter count or candidate-selection cost. Do not call it a fully compute-matched comparison.

Required controls: an expert intentionally trained with labels shifted by one modulo ten, one calibration source per capability, and an easy merge of three copies of one expert where the ordinary candidate already meets selection-derived floors (accuracy minus 0.03). Ablations disable deficit priority, variable rank, protection and correction, separately. Keep failed repairs. If no candidate meets all floors, return `no acceptable candidate found`; this is no proof of impossibility.

## Bounded text-adapter integration

Planned source: UCI SMS Spam Collection, Almeida & Hidalgo (2011), DOI 10.24432/C5CC84, CC BY 4.0, https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/228/sms+spam+collection . At initial registration automatic approval review rejected the download as outside the read-file request. The human then explicitly authorized the full attached objective, including this dataset download, local training/testing, committing/pushing and public release. The official UCI zip download then succeeded; its SHA256 is recorded. The original protocol is retained in study/pilot-v1/PROTOCOL.registration.md.

Deduplicate exact texts before stratified splitting; select equal ham/spam counts before the same 55/10/15/20 partition fractions with independent source seed 20261030. Fixed signed 256-bin UTF-8 byte unigram/bigram hashing uses BLAKE2b person MChordV1 and L2 normalization, case-sensitive with no fitted vocabulary. MLP 256→64→32→2, labels [ham, spam]. Base: 160 full-batch Adam epochs at 0.004 on original strings. Specialists train only rank-4 additive factors on uppercase, leetspeak, and underscore-spaced domains for 100 epochs at 0.008; all base weights and biases remain frozen. Export safe-tensor adapters and folded dense experts. Text floors: clean 0.75, transformed domains 0.72; protected clean regression allowance 0.03. Select ordinary arithmetic on the same scalar grid and attempt the same bounded repair. This is a character-feature classifier integration, not an LLM benchmark. Never mix text and vision artifacts. Do not redistribute raw SMS text.

## Confirmation and measurements

Before any confirmation metrics are computed, write a lock of protocol, data/split ledgers, all recipe selections, source numerical files and every artifact weight hash. Only construct confirmation batches after that lock. Any source/recipe revision after seeing final results requires a new untouched confirmation set. Source code for numerical methods freezes with the lock; CLI and interface source changes are irrelevant to numerical recipes and are excluded.

Report each capability's accuracy, cross entropy, ten-bin expected calibration error and examples; fraction of floors met, worst deficit, protected regressions; calibration/selection/gradient/teacher counts; per-build/training wall time; peak process RSS (macOS bytes); bytes for all artifacts including heads and retained adapters; warm batch-one inference median/p95 over 100 repeats after 20 warmups. Ensemble storage and latency include all three experts and are quality/cost references, not equivalent-memory baselines. All attempted recipes remain registered, including insufficient-data/conflict and unsuccessful repair cases.

Limits: shared small digit source distribution, artificial domain transformations, small confirmation sample, unavailable writer identities, only two expert initializations, limited adapter-classifier integration, no claims about natural multi-dataset transfer, foundation models, universal reliability or machine unlearning. No external pretraining was used here; that does not establish contamination-free performance for future imported experts. Malformed/nonfinite/extreme-input and altered-contract tests check software behavior; they do not certify adversarial ML robustness. Dataset source content is CC BY 4.0 and must remain attributed. Locally authored code and trained weights are MIT.

## Pre-confirmation amendment, before any final result

The original inversion specialist schedule (65 Adam epochs at 0.006) improved selection accuracy from 0.000 to only 0.200 on seed 11, and from 0.011 to 0.337 on seed 29. It was a weak third specialist. Preserve this pilot, its candidate registry, negative repairs and 100/200/300/400-epoch diagnostics in the study ledger. For the final main suite, use a fixed 1,200-epoch Adam schedule at 0.001 for the inversion expert, starting from the same common base. Keep every other expert/base schedule, task, floor, source partition, and repair budget unchanged. This amendment was made after calibration/selection diagnostics and before any confirmation metrics. No final-set revision occurred. Seed 29 is held out from the final merge-scalar selection, but earlier pilot diagnostics mean it is not wholly unseen development evidence.

Repair the strongest ordinary method selected by the same worst-deficit objective as an additional candidate whenever it differs from task arithmetic. Keep the conventional task-arithmetic repair demonstration. Compare an additional ordinary joint-mixture full-weight fine-tuning baseline: each repair gradient step is replaced by a step on the same number of examples sampled without replacement from the pooled four calibration domains, at fixed RNG seed 8801; match total accesses, steps and zero teacher calls. The original target-sequence matched full-weight baseline is retained and named `target_matched_finetune`, separate from `joint_finetune`. Both use the declared three-learning-rate grid and all attempts remain registered. Neither has matched exact FLOPs/candidate evaluation cost.

## Final pre-confirmation accounting clarification

Clean is also protected against more than 0.10 accuracy-point regression relative to the base on the same partition. For these bases the fixed 0.88 clean floor is at least as strong on selection; both the clean-vs-base regression and clean-vs-ordinary regression are reported separately. No floor was lowered. RegMean has zero distillation-teacher calls but does execute three parent-expert activation forwards, covering 3×179=537 calibration example accesses per main build. Report those separately from selection/repair counts. Repair itself has zero parent-expert forwards. Every recipe now has a unique pilot/main/text/diagnostic identity and immutable artifact manifest, weights and report hashes; source specialist training attempts are included. A same-start ordinary pooled-domain joint fine-tuning comparator is added for strongest-baseline repair with the exact same gradient-step count, total example accesses and zero teacher calls, selected from the same declared three learning rates.
