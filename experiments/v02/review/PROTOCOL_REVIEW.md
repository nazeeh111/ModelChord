# Independent v0.2 protocol review

Reviewed 2026-10-29 against frozen v0.1.0 commit `36adfda70544d7af2ff9ffe22f70cd4a8fc48fa8`, its objective, `TASK_STATE.md`, `docs/PROTOCOL.md`, `docs/RESULTS.md`, `scripts/run_study.py`, `src/modelchord/repair.py`, and the explicit MLP implementation. This review did not read prospective confirmation examples, download data, train models, alter frozen files, or inspect new confirmation metrics.

## Decision

Proceed with a separately versioned experiment, subject to the gates below. Training rank-limited factors is a defensible next hypothesis. v1 proposed one normalized gradient direction for one layer per accepted round and allowed at most four rounds. Its failure does not establish that a trained low-rank correction lacks capacity. Conversely, more training alone is not evidence that deficit prioritization or targeted repair is necessary. A strong ordinary joint fine-tuning result may explain all improvement.

Use a development-only capacity test before spending the new confirmation set. Ask whether this architecture can meet the unchanged contract at all under ordinary joint learning, whether factor training can approach that result, and then whether deficit/protection/allocation choices improve an equally budgeted factor baseline. Keep failed tests. Do not lower floors to create a success.

## Material design risks

1. **New examples are not a new benchmark.** The unused original UCI training subset can serve as newly reserved confirmation only if its bytes/examples were never used in expert training, development, candidate selection, or earlier evaluation. It remains the same historical collection and artificial domains. Reusing the old bases/experts and both exposed seeds supports an unseen-source-example test, not an unseen-expert, unseen-task, unseen-seed, or independent-dataset claim. The metadata relationship between the original 3,823 and 1,797 subsets needs an authoritative source before any writer-disjoint claim.
2. **Cross-source leakage must include transformed views.** Hash canonical raw pixels across both source files before applying the registered exclusion rule. Also audit transformed-input collisions across development and confirmation, and collisions with conflicting labels within each evaluated domain. A source-ID disjointness check alone can miss identical transformed inputs. Do not choose exclusions from model mistakes or remove hard cases after results. Publish raw counts, group/exclusion counts, retained class counts, and the precise rule. Source deduplication is not writer independence.
3. **Changing the split silently changes the hypothesis.** Keep all old confirmation IDs excluded from v0.2 development even though the aggregate results have been seen. The original 1,797 records outside those IDs may retain their old train/calibration/selection roles. Reassigning old expert-training examples to validation would make validation optimistic because the experts already learned them. If more data are permitted for repair, identify whether they were expert-training data and separate the resulting experiment from the calibration-only claim.
4. **An accuracy deficit is not a differentiable training loss.** Define separately the smooth loss used for gradient updates and the lexicographic validation objective used for checkpoint acceptance. A maximum of raw cross-entropies can target a high-entropy task that already meets its accuracy floor. State how accuracy deficits, smoothing, loss scale, clipping, and protection weights enter the update. Keep the protection reference fixed to the ordinary merge/base as registered, rather than allowing incremental regression to accumulate across accepted checkpoints.
5. **Factor learning can be ineffective for implementation reasons.** For `Delta W = B A`, initializing both factors to zero yields zero factor gradients. Use a declared nondegenerate initialization with zero initial correction, finite-value checks, and a development check that one step changes the intended factors and logits. Total deployed rank is the sum of per-layer correction ranks, not the rank of one update or the number of trainable tensors. Keep biases frozen or explicitly count and disclose any bias training as a changed method. Folded dense weights must match the factor model on changed inputs.
6. **Hard gates can reject useful trajectories.** Checking only an immediate one-step proposal may block a later feasible checkpoint. A bounded factor-training trajectory can retain all predeclared checkpoints and select among those satisfying the fixed protection contract. Internal optimizer steps may violate a margin; accepted/exported checkpoints must not. This distinction must be registered before running. Never reset the protected reference after accepting a checkpoint.
7. **Validation is a resource and an overfitting channel.** A much larger checkpoint/layer/rank search on the same small selection set can manufacture apparent floor attainment. Freeze trial count, learning rates, rank allocations, checkpoint schedule, seed policy, tie-breaks, stopping rule and treatment of failed/nonfinite runs. Log every attempted trial. Do not adapt the protocol after new confirmation has been opened.
8. **Novelty is not established by extra optimization.** Trained additive low-rank factors resemble established adapter fine-tuning. The defensible contribution is the measured contract, bounded selection procedure and static artifact integration, with any deficit/protection advantage supported by controlled comparisons. Local intervention effects are evidence of candidate effects at that checkpoint; they do not prove a general causal theory of interference.

## Minimum comparison design

- Keep the same selected ordinary starting weights for repair and its main joint comparator, separately for seeds 11 and 29. Report the inherited ordinary-build cost, including RegMean parent activation calls. Retain task arithmetic as the original conventional reference. Do not allow repair alone to pick its best initialization after seeing confirmation.
- Run an ordinary pooled-domain full-weight joint fine-tuning comparator with a competent optimizer, a predeclared learning-rate search, and the same allowed source examples, transformed views, teacher allowance, training-example-access ceiling, selection-checkpoint ceiling, and seed policy. Four-step SGD is not a sufficient comparator for hundreds of Adam factor steps.
- Run ordinary fixed-rank factor fine-tuning from the same start, using the same architecture, total rank, initialization, optimization budget and selection gate. Uniform pooled-domain loss versus deficit-directed loss is the essential mechanism comparison. A full-weight comparison alone confounds the objective and parameterization.
- For priority, allocation and protection ablations, preserve total data accesses, optimizer steps, trial count and validation access ceiling. Record per-task accesses and weighting separately. If a removed feature allows a different feasible search space, say so. Never inherit v1's 537-versus-2,864-access ablation mismatch.
- Give both methods an equal total-build resource ceiling and also show matched-gradient-budget results if exact compute matching is infeasible. Include all unsuccessful hyperparameter trials, initialization/candidate diagnostics, validation inference, decompositions, parent calls and checkpoint exports in the cost ledger. Equal update count is not equal work: full-weight and factor backpropagation have different costs. Use measured consistent profiling or label the ceiling as wall-time matching with its limitations; do not claim exact FLOP matching without measuring it.
- Before confirmation, retain a small development-only paired intervention check: apply the proposed candidate and an equal-norm non-targeted/fixed-subspace correction at the same state, evaluate all four capabilities, and log effects. Include a uniform-factor comparator regardless. This checks the proposed direction rather than assuming gradient similarity diagnoses interference. It does not need to become another large search.

## Concrete acceptance gates

Before any confirmation inference:

1. Verify all 327 frozen paths against the original lock. Write v0.2 source, ledgers, artifacts and reports only outside those paths. Verification must include byte hashes, not just a clean git status.
2. Freeze a standalone protocol and recipe registry, canonical data provenance, allowed/excluded source IDs, duplicate/collision policy, exact floors, references, resource limits, all attempted trials, selected model hashes, numerical source hashes, and deterministic confirmation command. Record the source of writer-split metadata. Confirmation data access is not permitted for debugging preprocessing or testing the evaluator before this lock; use development fixtures.
3. The selected candidate must satisfy every floor and protected-reference constraint on selection: clean at least 0.88, left/right/inverted at least 0.80, clean no more than 0.02 below its selected ordinary reference, and no more than 0.10 below base. Use exact counts for decisions; rounded percentages cannot cross a gate. Preserve these v1 protection limits unless a distinctly labeled new contract is registered beforehand.
4. Check the rank ceiling and trained-parameter accounting, zero initial factor effect, finite outputs, factor/folded-logit equivalence, compatible manifest/preprocessing, changed-input behavior, and a static export that loads with all parent artifacts unavailable. Do not count a deployed dense tensor's numerical rank as the correction rank; check the declared additive difference against its factor representation.
5. Confirm all budget ceilings and match reports from machine-readable counters. A run exceeding a ceiling is invalid for that comparison even if accurate. A failed candidate is not silently omitted or replaced after confirmation.

On one locked confirmation run:

6. Evaluate all preregistered candidates/references on the same retained source groups and domains. To claim an accepted composed candidate, it must pass its selection gate first and meet all unchanged floors and protected-reference constraints on confirmation. Keep a failed-selection model unaccepted even if it passes confirmation, as v1 did for text. Report the measured frontier if none qualifies.
7. Report per-domain exact counts, accuracy, cross entropy, calibration, floors met, worst deficit, both clean regressions, build/selection/training costs, stored bytes, and actual static-inference latency. Resample source groups jointly across transformed domains for confidence intervals; do not treat four views of an image as four independent people. Intervals describe uncertainty and must not be used to retrofit an unregistered acceptance test. If population-level floor confidence is claimed, preregister a simultaneous one-sided bound gate instead of relying on point estimates.
8. Distinguish three outcomes: retention succeeds if the composed candidate meets the frozen contract; repair improves over its start if paired deficits improve; mechanism superiority requires the matched fixed-rank/uniform and joint comparisons to support it. A joint baseline also meeting floors does not negate artifact retention, but it prevents claiming repair was necessary. No claim of superiority, compute efficiency or broad generalization follows from two reused seeds and one source collection alone.

After this run, any revised numerical method or recipe requires another genuinely untouched confirmation source. Preserve v0.1.0 as its negative locked study, regardless of v0.2 results. A vision-only v0.2 result must not imply that the original bounded text-adapter integration has newly passed selection or has been reconfirmed.
